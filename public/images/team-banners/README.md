# Team banner backgrounds

`rink.png` (Fresh ice) and `equipment.png` (Ready to play) were AI-created for this app on October 19, 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool. They depict equipment/empty ice, with no real people, logos or team claims. Composition leaves dark space for the team title and keeps the subject within a short header crop. Coaches can choose a background or privately upload their own image and adjust the focus.
